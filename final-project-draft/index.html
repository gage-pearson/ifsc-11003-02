Before Your Trip

Here are some tips to help you prepare for your adventure in Arkansas:

  • Check the weather forecast before you go.
  • Bring appropriate gear and clothing for the conditions.
  • Let someone know your plans and expected return time.
  • Stay on designated trails and follow Leave No Trace principles.
